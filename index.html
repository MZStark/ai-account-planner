From company name to account strategy.

Method

Built to be trusted in front of a customer.

Facts and hypotheses apart

What the company does today carries its source. What it could do is labelled as a hypothesis and tied to a strategic priority.

Priorities before products

Every use case starts from something the company is publicly trying to achieve, then maps to API, ChatGPT Enterprise or Codex.

Adoption and impact

A deal is the start. Each strategy defines governance, the metrics leadership will track and how usage grows from land to scale.

About

Why this exists.

Account Directors own a dozen key accounts. For each, they need a clear view of where AI fits the company's strategy, who decides, and how usage grows after the first deal. This planner does the research and refuses to invent.

Built by Mehdi Zerraf as a working simulation of that job.

Personal demo project, not affiliated with OpenAI or any company shown. Strategies rely on public sources only and must be checked before use.
